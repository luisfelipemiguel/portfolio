### Internship

# Cybersecurity Awareness Training for the Company Employees

## Objective
Develop and deliver a basic cybersecurity awareness training for company employees, focusing on fundamental concepts such as malware, social engineering, and effective self-protection techniques. The project was conceived and executed in the context of a student internship and included the creation of a comprehensive presentation, with all content in English, using free Text-to-Speech (TTS) tools.
